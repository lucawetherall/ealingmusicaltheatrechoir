About the Choir

Where Passion Meets the Stage

Ealing Musical Theatre Choir is a vibrant adult choir that celebrates the magic of musical theatre — from Sondheim to Schwartz, Hamilton to Les Misérables. We believe great singing starts with great joy, not great credentials.

Led by Musical Director Izzy Mohan — graduate of the University of Oxford and the Royal Academy of Music — our choir offers a professional musical experience in a warm, inclusive setting.
