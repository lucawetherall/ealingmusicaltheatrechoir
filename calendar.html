Weekly Rehearsals

Come Sing With Us

We rehearse weekly throughout the year (excluding school holidays). All are welcome to come along to a taster session — no booking required.

What to Expect

  • A warm welcome from Izzy and the choir
  • A focused, fun rehearsal led by a professional Musical Director
  • Sheet music provided — no need to bring anything
  • No sight-reading skills required; we learn by ear and by printed music
  • Friendly people who share your love of musical theatre
  • Tea & coffee (because every good choir needs it!)

🎟️ New members: Your first taster session is free. Come along, see if you love it, then decide.
