Our Story

About the Choir

Passion, community, and a love for the music that moves us — that's what Ealing Musical Theatre Choir is all about.

Who We Are

A Choir Built on Joy

Ealing Musical Theatre Choir was founded to bring together West Londoners who share a deep love of musical theatre — from the sweeping ballads of Les Misérables to the exhilarating energy of Hamilton, from golden-age classics to the newest West End hits.

We are an adult choir open to everyone. You don't need to have sung in a choir before. You don't need to read music. You just need to turn up with enthusiasm, an open heart, and a willingness to give it your all.

Our rehearsals are fun, focused, and professionally led. And when concert time comes, we perform alongside professional musical theatre instrumentalists — giving every member a truly extraordinary performance experience.

Musical Direction

Meet Izzy Mohan

Izzy Mohan

Musical Director, Ealing Musical Theatre Choir

Izzy Mohan is a passionate conductor and music educator whose career spans the West End, youth arts, and community choral practice. With world-class academic and conservatoire training, Izzy brings extraordinary depth of musicianship to every rehearsal — alongside a natural warmth and ability to bring out the best in every singer.

● Graduate of the University of Oxford
● Graduate of the Royal Academy of Music
● Musical Director, British Youth Music Theatre (BYMT)
● Choir Director across multiple London choirs

"I believe everyone has a musical voice worth hearing. My job is simply to help you find it — and to make the journey as joyful as possible."

What We Believe

Our Values

Everything we do is guided by three simple principles.

♪

Joy First

Singing should be exhilarating. We create an environment where every rehearsal feels like a celebration of music and community.

◇

Radical Inclusion

No auditions, no prerequisites. If you love musical theatre and want to sing, you belong here. Full stop.

✦

Professional Excellence

We hold ourselves to a high musical standard — and our concerts with professional instrumentalists reflect that ambition.
