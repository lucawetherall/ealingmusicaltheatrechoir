Just Passion.

Ealing Musical Theatre Choir is open to any adult who loves musical theatre. There's no audition, no minimum skill level — we just want people who are excited to sing.

Getting Started

How It Works

Joining is simple. Here's what to expect from your first session to your first performance.

1

Come Along

Turn up to any rehearsal for a free taster session. No booking needed — just show up and sing.

2

Learn the Music

Izzy will guide you through the repertoire. Sheet music is provided. Learning tracks may also be shared to practice at home.

3

Take the Stage

At concerts, you'll perform with professional musical theatre instrumentalists to an audience that can feel the magic you've created together.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you come along.

Do I need to audition?

▾

Absolutely not. Ealing Musical Theatre Choir has no audition process. We welcome any adult who loves musical theatre and wants to sing. Your first step is simply turning up to a rehearsal.

What experience do I need?

▾

None whatsoever. You don't need to have sung in a choir before, and you don't need to read music. Our Musical Director Izzy teaches the music by ear and with printed scores, so everyone can participate at their own level.

What kind of music do you sing?

▾

We focus on musical theatre — from classic golden-age shows like West Side Story and Guys and Dolls to contemporary hits like Hamilton, Dear Evan Hansen, and the latest West End releases. The repertoire changes each term.

When and where are rehearsals?

▾

We rehearse weekly in Ealing, West London. Full details — including the day, time, and venue address — are on the Rehearsals & Concerts page.

How much does membership cost?

▾

Your first taster session is free. After that, a termly membership fee applies to cover venue hire, sheet music, and musical direction. The exact fee is listed above, or you can email us for current pricing. We are committed to keeping singing accessible — if cost is a concern, please reach out.

Do I have to perform in the concerts?

▾

We hope you will — it's one of the most exciting parts of being in the choir! That said, there is no obligation. If life gets in the way and you can't make a concert, that's completely fine. Consistent rehearsal attendance is what we ask for most.

Who leads the choir?

▾

Ealing Musical Theatre Choir is directed by Izzy Mohan, a graduate of the University of Oxford and the Royal Academy of Music. Izzy also works with British Youth Music Theatre and directs choirs across London. You're in excellent hands. Learn more about Izzy →
